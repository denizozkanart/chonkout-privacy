Chonk Out — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-30

Chonk Out ("the Game") is developed and published by Kyro Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Game handles and why.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not ask for or store your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, location, or any account credentials. The Game has no chat and no user-to-user interaction. Game progress is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its mediated ad networks. To serve and measure ads, these partners may process:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / GAID on Android), only with your permission where the platform requires it (App Tracking Transparency on iOS)
  • Coarse, non-precise region (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction events (views, taps)

You can withdraw tracking permission at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). Ads still work without the identifier; they are simply less relevant.

Partner policies:

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource: https://www.is.com/privacy-policy/
  • Additional mediated networks, when enabled, are listed in the LevelPlay partner documentation.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase receipts are validated by the platform.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the Game evolves; the date above reflects the latest revision.
