# Chonk Out — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-17_

Chonk Out ("the Game") is developed and published by Kyro Games ("we", "us").
This policy explains what data the Game handles and why.

## Data we do NOT collect
We do not ask for or store your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos,
location, or any account credentials. The Game has no chat and no user-to-user
interaction. Game progress is stored only on your device.

## Children
The Game is made for a mixed audience that includes children under 13, and we
treat every player as a child. That means:

- Ads are contextual only. They are never personalised, and no interest-based
  targeting or remarketing is used.
- No advertising identifier is collected or transmitted — not the Android
  advertising ID (AAID) and not the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA). The
  Android build ships without the advertising-ID permission.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and with
the Google Play Families policy.

## Advertising
The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its mediated ad
networks. To serve and measure ads, these partners may process:

- Device information (model, OS version, language)
- Coarse, non-precise region (derived from IP address)
- Ad interaction events (views, taps)

The Game does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, and it
does not use an advertising identifier. On iOS it does not request the App
Tracking Transparency permission. Ads are chosen from the context of this game
alone, never from your activity elsewhere. Our ad partners are configured for
child-directed treatment, and ad content is restricted to a general audience
rating.

Partner policies:
- Unity LevelPlay / ironSource: https://www.is.com/privacy-policy/
- Additional mediated networks, when enabled, are listed in the LevelPlay
  partner documentation.

## In-app purchases
Purchases are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never
see or store your payment details. Purchase receipts are validated by the
platform.

## Deleting your data
All game progress lives on your device, so uninstalling the Game deletes it.
To request deletion of any data held by our advertising partners, write to
dozkanart@gmail.com and we will pass the request on. We answer within 30 days.

## Changes
We may update this policy as the Game evolves; the date above reflects the
latest revision.
